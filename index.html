Welcome to Teddy Bear's Playroom!

About Us
Welcome to Teddy Bear’s Playroom, a cozy play space in Paragould, Arkansas, created with our youngest friends in mind! Designed for children 10 and under, Teddy Bear’s Playroom is a place where kids can play, explore, imagine, and make memories in a fun and welcoming environment. While we’re currently growing in a smaller space, we’re proud to offer open play where little ones can simply be themselves and enjoy some time to play.
Come Play With Us!
We currently offer open play from 9 AM–5 PM on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays. We’re closed on Wednesdays, and every Sunday we host a special weekly event from 2–4 PM. Be sure to follow along to see what fun we have planned! We also believe that every child deserves a place to feel comfortable and included. That’s why we offer sensory accommodations for friends who may need them, helping make Teddy Bear’s Playroom a welcoming experience for as many children as possible.
Our future
Teddy Bear’s Playroom may be small right now, but we have big dreams! Our goal is to grow into a larger space where we can expand our offerings and become a play center for all ages—giving our older friends a place to play, connect, and have fun, too. We’re so excited to grow alongside the families of Paragould and the surrounding communities. Thank you for being part of the Teddy Bear’s Playroom family and helping us turn our little playroom into something even bigger. Come play, explore, and make some memories with us!
